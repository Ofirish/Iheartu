Your shared heart

Everything the heart can do. Most of it is a secret gesture, so try them together.

The basics

Touch the heartSends glowing ripples and sparks to both phones, and a short buzz to the other person. Slide your finger to keep sending.
Both touch at onceWithin about a second and a half of each other (or both holding), a glowing pink and purple "I love you" appears on both phones. If it's night for either of you, it says "Good night my love" with a big kiss instead.
Invite someoneThe button shares your room link. It disappears for good once two people have shared the room.
Status lineShows whether the other heart is here, waiting or reconnecting. Double tap it to see your achievements.

How the heart behaves

RestingAfter a minute without a touch the heart turns dull and stops pulsing, on both phones. A touch wakes it.
NightFrom 22:00 to 07:00 for either of you, the background becomes a starry galaxy and the heart beats very slowly. Buzzes are muted when it's night for you.
While you were awayYour next touch shows how many times you were loved while you were gone. It never says zero.

Kisses and love

Double tap the heartSends kisses 💋 floating up on both phones.
Kiss rainTouch the bottom tip, then the left and right top bulges (either order) within four seconds. The background fills with kisses for 10 seconds on both phones.
I love you tooWhile "I love you" is showing, double tap the heart to answer "I love you too".
I love you rainHold one finger on the empty space left of the heart and one on the empty space right of it at the same time. "I love you", hugs, hearts and kisses float across the background for 10 seconds on both phones.
Answer the rainTouch one of the floating "I love you"s and the background switches to "I love you too".
Good girlStroke the heart slowly with one finger. Both phones show "Good girl" with a soft glow and a gentle buzz.
Finger heartTap with two fingers close together for a little burst of hearts.

Magic tricks

PatronusFlick one finger quickly sideways. A glowing silver-blue stag gallops across both screens.
LightsabersTouch with two fingers on opposite sides of the heart. Blue and red ripples cross with a humming buzz.
HyperspaceAt night, swipe quickly upward. The stars stretch into light-speed streaks.
8-bit heartTap the empty space above, above, below, below, left, right, left, right of the heart, one tap each. The heart turns into pixel art for 10 seconds on both phones.
Binary heartHold the centre of the heart for two seconds. It fills with 0s and 1s for 10 seconds.
ConfettiShake the phone three times. Confetti hearts rain down on both phones.
Cheesy lineTriple tap one spot on the empty background for a random pick-up line on both phones.

Notes, dates and achievements

Hidden noteHold the bottom tip of the heart for one second, type a short note and tap "Hide note". Your partner sees it the next time they open the heart. Notes only wait about twelve hours.
AnniversaryTap the bottom-left corner once to set your date. Your partner's phone gets it too. On the day the heart glows gold and says "Happy anniversary".
AchievementsThe heart quietly counts the hearts you send. At milestones such as 20, 50, 100 and 500 a 🏆 message shows for ten seconds. Double tap the status line to see what you've unlocked. The numbers of the locked ones stay a secret.
StreakWhen you both touch the heart on the same day for 7, 30, 100 or 365 days in a row, a 🔥 message appears.

Keeping it fresh

RefreshTap the bottom-right corner to clear the saved copy and load the newest version of the heart. Your room is kept.
Your roomThe room is remembered on your phone and never expires. To start a different one, use a private window or open someone else's invite link.
Add to home screenIn Chrome on Android, open the menu and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". It then opens full screen, and swiping down from the top shows the system bars.

Tip: the effects marked "on both phones" are sent through the shared room, so you both see them at the same time.
